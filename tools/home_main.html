Questions people ask.

Do I need to know how to edit?

No. ClipHeaven finds, cuts and captions the clips. You only post them.

Does it post for me?

No, on purpose. You post every clip yourself, which keeps your accounts within TikTok's, Instagram's and YouTube's rules. ClipHeaven gets the clip and caption ready so posting takes seconds.

Which streamers can I clip?

Any public Twitch or Kick streamer you choose. Every clip credits the streamer's channel. Always follow each platform's rules and respect a streamer's wishes about clipping.

Do I need a computer?

No. Everything runs in the cloud and you use the app on your phone. Setup takes about 30–40 minutes once, with the step-by-step guide.

How many clips will I get?

You choose. Around 20 a day is a good target for a growing page, and you can make more or fewer.
