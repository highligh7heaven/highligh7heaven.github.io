Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

ClipHeaven is a personal tool used only by its owner. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, ClipHeaven uses two Google permissions:

  • Google Drive (only files it creates): to make a "ClipHeaven Clips" folder and save finished videos, captions and reports in it. It cannot see or change any other files in the Drive.
  • Google Sheets (read only): to read the owner's ClipHeaven settings spreadsheet.

What it does with it

The data is used only to make and save the owner's videos. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. Nothing is stored outside the owner's own Google Drive and the owner's private GitHub account.

Removing access

The owner can remove ClipHeaven's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/highligh7heaven.
