Set up ClipHeaven in about 30 minutes.

You do this once, from your phone or a computer. Every step is free, and the app checks your setup for you.

What you'll need

  • A phone or computer
  • A Google account
  • A GitHub account
  • A Twitch account
  • Your ClipHeaven licence (from Whop)
  1. 1

    Make your own copy 2 min

    Accept the GitHub invite you get after buying, then make your own copy (a "fork") of ClipHeaven with one tap and switch on its Actions.

  2. 2

    Add your licence key 1 min

    Copy the licence key from your Whop membership and save it in your copy as a secret.

  3. 3

    Connect Twitch 5 min

    Register a free app at dev.twitch.tv and save its two keys. This lets ClipHeaven find streamers' clips.

  4. 4

    Connect Google Drive 15 min

    The fiddly one, but you only do it once: a free Google Cloud project so finished clips can save to your Drive.

  5. 5

    Turn on AI titles 2 min

    Optional but recommended: a free Gemini key so ClipHeaven can watch clips, score them and write titles.

  6. 6

    Check and run 5 min

    Run the setup check, pick your streamers in the app, then make your first test clip.

Buyers get the full guide, with every tap written out, in their copy of ClipHeaven.

Fixing common problems.

A run stopped with a red cross

Open the app: failed runs show what went wrong and how to fix it, often with a one-tap fix. Most often a key was pasted with a space at the end.

No clips showed up

Check you've picked streamers in Settings and that they streamed recently. Quiet streamers can give fewer clips; try looking back more days.

Google says "This app isn't verified"

That's normal for your own Google Cloud project. Tap Advanced, then Go to ClipHeaven. Only you use it.

Runs stopped after a while

GitHub's free minutes may have run out for the month. The app's Stats tab shows your minutes, when they reset and your options.

Still stuck?

Take a screenshot of what you see and get in touch from the contact page.
